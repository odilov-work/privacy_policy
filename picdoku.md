# Privacy Policy for Picdoku

**Effective date:** July 22, 2026  
**Last updated:** July 22, 2026

Picdoku (the **“App”**) is provided by **Odilov Ahrorbek**
(**“we,” “us,”** or **“our”**). This Privacy Policy explains how information is
handled when you use the App.

## Summary

Picdoku is an offline Sudoku game. The current version:

- does not require or provide user accounts;
- does not collect or transmit personal information to us;
- does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs;
- does not track users across apps or websites; and
- does not sell or share personal information.

## Information stored on your device

The App stores information locally on your device so that its features work as
expected. This may include:

- game and challenge progress;
- saved games and gameplay statistics;
- tutorial progress;
- hint balance;
- awards and completion records;
- language selection; and
- audio, vibration, and board-display preferences.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us. Depending
on your operating-system and device settings, locally stored App information may
be included in backups managed by Apple, Google, or your device provider. Those
backups are governed by the provider's own terms and privacy policy.

## Device permissions and capabilities

The App may use the following device capabilities:

- **Vibration:** to provide optional gameplay feedback. On Android, the App
  requests vibration permission. This permission does not provide access to
  personal information.
- **Audio playback:** to play locally bundled music and sound effects. The App
  does not access or record your microphone.

The current version does not request access to your location, contacts, camera,
photos, microphone, or advertising identifier.

## Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The current version of Picdoku does not integrate an advertising network, does
not serve personalized advertisements, and does not use analytics or
cross-app tracking technologies.

If a future version introduces advertising, analytics, crash reporting,
purchases, accounts, cloud sync, or another feature that transmits information
off your device, we will update this Privacy Policy and any required store
privacy disclosures before that feature is released.

## Third-party software and platforms

The App uses open-source software libraries to provide functions such as the
user interface, local storage, audio playback, and vibration. In the current
version, these libraries are used for on-device functionality and are not
configured by us to transmit personal information.

If you download the App through Apple App Store or Google Play, the store
provider may independently process information related to downloads, purchases,
device diagnostics, or store usage. We do not control that processing. Please
review the applicable provider's privacy policy:

- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Data sharing and sale

We do not collect personal information through the current version of the App.
Accordingly, we do not sell, rent, disclose, or share personal information with
third parties.

## Data retention and deletion

Because gameplay information is stored locally, we do not have access to it and
cannot retrieve or delete it remotely. You can remove locally stored App data by
clearing the App's storage through your device settings or by uninstalling the
App. Your operating-system backup settings may separately retain a backup copy.

## Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from
any other users. If you believe that information has been provided to us outside
the App, please contact us using the details below.

## Security

We design the App so that gameplay information remains on your device. However,
no device or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are
responsible for maintaining the security of your device and operating-system
account.

## Your privacy rights

Because the current version does not send personal information to us, we do not
hold App-related personal information that we can access, correct, export, or
delete. You can control or delete the locally stored information as described in
the **Data retention and deletion** section.

If you contact us directly, we will use the information in your message only to
respond to your request, comply with legal obligations, and protect our rights.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, data practices, or
legal obligations change. We will update the effective date above and provide
additional notice when required by applicable law.
